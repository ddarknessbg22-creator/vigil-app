Overcoming temptation
An urge is a visitor, not a resident. It will leave.|You do not have to win the feeling. You only have to outlast it.|Name it: urge. Then let it be a thing you observe, not obey.|The spike lasts minutes. The decision lasts a lifetime.|When the impulse arrives, change rooms before changing your mind.
Temptation loves an open tab and an idle hand.|You are allowed to want something and still say no.|The urge does not know your reasons. You do.|Do not argue with craving. Give it somewhere else to go.|Every pause you create is a small victory.
Take three breaths before you touch anything.|The feeling is not a command.|Delay is not denial. Give it ten minutes and watch it shrink.|If your brain says one more, ask: one more what?|Put friction between you and the click.
You can be tempted and still be in control.|A craving is uncomfortable, not dangerous.|Do not test your willpower. Remove the opportunity.|The urge you ignore becomes the urge you forget.|Choose the version of you who does not need this.
When in doubt, step outside.|Hunger, anger, boredom, loneliness: check those first.|Your job is not to feel pure. It is to act aligned.|Watch the urge rise, peak and fall without joining it.|Say no once, calmly, and mean it.
Temptation is a wave. You do not have to surf it.|Make the easy thing the right thing.|You are not weak for feeling it. You are strong for not following it.|A single moment of clarity can undo months of autopilot.|Leave the room. Change the story. Keep the day.
The second no is easier than the first.
