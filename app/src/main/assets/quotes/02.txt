When an urge hits
An urge is a wave. Do not make it a decision.|Wait ten minutes. Most urges expire on their own.|You do not have to win the feeling, only outlast it.|Change rooms. Change the story. Keep the day.
The spike is short. The regret is long.|Name it: urge. Then let it pass through.|Boredom is not an emergency. Find something else.|Do not negotiate with a craving at midnight.
Put friction between you and the click.|The feeling is weather. You are the sky.|Do not feed it. It will leave.|Hunger, tiredness, loneliness — check those first.
You have survived every urge you ever had.|One more minute of not giving in is a win.|Walk outside. The urge loses focus.|The impulse is loud, but it is not your voice.
Do not test your willpower. Remove the chance.|Strong people still feel it. They just do not follow.|This moment is not worth your streak.|Breathe. This too will pass.
