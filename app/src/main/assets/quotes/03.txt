After a setback
A setback is a data point, not a verdict.|You are not back at zero. You are back with knowledge.|Come back sooner than last time. That is progress.|The days you kept were real. Nobody can take them.
Start again from now. Not from tomorrow.|Forgive yourself quickly, then learn slowly.|Guilt is heavy and useless. Use information instead.|Your worth never moved. Only the clock did.
Every comeback makes the next one easier.|One slip does not cancel a month of choices.|Do not build an identity out of one bad night.|The best run you had also began with one decision.
Begin again. That is the whole skill.|You are allowed to be human and keep going.|What triggered it? Change the route, not the goal.|Your next clean day is more important than last night.
No shame. Just the next right choice.|You are still someone who keeps showing up.|Start smaller if you need to. Just start.|The past is a lesson, not a life sentence.
