# Credits & sources

## Original content
The majority of the 2,300+ lines in `app/src/main/assets/quotes/` are original VIGIL writing, written for this app. They are marked **VIGIL original** in the app.

## Public-domain and attributed quotations
`30.txt` (category: Mental strength) contains quotations from public-domain authors. Each line carries its attribution inline after ` ~~ `, and the app shows that source beneath the quotation. Sources include Marcus Aurelius (*Meditations*), Seneca (*Letters from a Stoic*), Buddha, Lao Tzu (*Tao Te Ching*), Confucius (*Analects*), William Shakespeare, Horace, Mark Twain, Ralph Waldo Emerson/Will Durant, Vince Lombardi, Thomas Edison, Nelson Mandela, Peter Drucker, James Clear, Jon Kabat-Zinn and Anne Lamott.

These are short quotations used with attribution. The translations/paraphrases are condensed for the app; where a translation is under copyright, treat the line as a paraphrase rather than a verbatim quote.

## Anime-inspired lines
`31.txt` (category: Self-respect) contains **original lines written for VIGIL**, each explicitly marked ` ~~ inspired by <series>`. They are not quotations from the shows and do not reproduce dialogue. They acknowledge the themes of works including Spirited Away, My Hero Academia, Attack on Titan, Violet Evergarden, Fruits Basket, Your Name, Haikyuu, Mushishi, Kiki's Delivery Service, Fullmetal Alchemist, Naruto, Princess Mononoke, Steins;Gate, One Punch Man, Anohana, Hunter x Hunter, Re:Zero, Demon Slayer and Neon Genesis Evangelion.

## Architecture and code
The app is native Kotlin/Jetpack Compose. Icons are original vector paths drawn in `VigilIcons.kt`; the launcher icon is an original vector drawable. No third-party font, image or icon pack is bundled.
